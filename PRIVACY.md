# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Markdown Under (the Chrome extension and the desktop app) is built to keep your documents
on your own computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. Markdown Under does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information,
browsing history, document contents or usage data. There is no analytics, no tracking,
no advertising and no account.

## What stays on your device

- **Your documents** are read and formatted locally, in your browser or in the desktop app.
  They are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Your settings** (theme, font, text size, page width and similar preferences) are saved
  with Chrome's built-in storage. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome may sync these settings
  between your own devices. We never see them.

## Network requests

Markdown Under makes no network requests of its own. If a document you open links to
images on the web, your browser loads those images the same way it would on any web
page, so the site hosting the image may see that request.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: to remember your settings.
- **Access to `.md` files** (on your computer and on websites): so the extension can
  recognise Markdown files and display them formatted. Access to files on your computer
  only works if you turn on "Allow access to file URLs" yourself.
