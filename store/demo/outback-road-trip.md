# Outback Road Trip: Sydney to Uluru

Ten days, 2,800 km and one very dusty car. This is the plan, the packing list and
the maths for not running out of fuel between roadhouses. :kangaroo:

## The route

| Leg | From | To | Distance | Drive time |
|-----|------|----|---------:|-----------:|
| 1 | Sydney | Dubbo | 390 km | 4.5 h |
| 2 | Dubbo | Broken Hill | 750 km | 8 h |
| 3 | Broken Hill | Port Augusta | 415 km | 4.5 h |
| 4 | Port Augusta | Coober Pedy | 540 km | 5.5 h |
| 5 | Coober Pedy | Yulara (Uluru) | 735 km | 7.5 h |

> [!TIP]
> Book the Coober Pedy underground motel early. It stays a cool 24 °C
> even when it's 45 °C outside.

> [!WARNING]
> Don't drive between dusk and dawn. Kangaroos, wombats and cattle are on the road,
> and most rental agreements don't cover animal strikes at night.

## Fuel maths

The longest gap between fuel stops is about 250 km. With consumption $c$ in litres
per 100 km and a tank of $T$ litres, the safe range (keeping a 20% reserve) is:

$$
R = \frac{0.8\,T}{c} \times 100
$$

For our car, $T = 60$ and $c = 9.5$, so $R \approx 505$ km. Plenty of margin, but
fill up at every roadhouse anyway.

## At Uluru

Uluru is 348 m high and 9.4 km around the base[^walk]. The sunrise viewing area
fills up fast, so leave Yulara by **5:30 am**.

> [!NOTE]
> Uluru is sacred to the Aṉangu people. Photography is restricted at some
> sites. Follow the signs and the rangers' guidance.

[^walk]: The full base walk takes about 3.5 hours. Start early and carry water.
